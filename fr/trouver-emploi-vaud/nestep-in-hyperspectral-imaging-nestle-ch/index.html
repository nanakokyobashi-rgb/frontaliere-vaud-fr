Be a force for good and take your NeStep with Nestlé

NeStep is a unique 12-month immersive experience at Nestlé in Switzerland for fresh graduates who want to learn fast, grow with purpose and make an impact. From day one, you spend at least half of your time owning one impactful project, combined with structured learning and continuous mentoring to deliver meaningful results.

As a NeStep talent in Hyperspectral Imaging at Nestlé Research, your mission is to help Nestlé continue to build its expertise in hyperspectral imaging, turning first-hand experience into high-value applications and informed technology decisions. You will collaborate closely with imaging scientists, microbiology and food safety experts, data science and artificial intelligence specialists, and quality and operations teams. This is your opportunity to learn by doing, grow with support and shape the future you envision for yourself and for the world around you.

How you’ll make an impact as a NeStep talent in Hyperspectral Imaging

  • Developing and validating new hyperspectral imaging applications, to address diverse analytical challenges in raw materials and finished products.
  • Building spectral reference datasets and analytical workflows that combine hands-on lab experimentation with image processing, machine learning and artificial intelligence.
  • Shaping a decision framework that helps Nestlé identify, prioritize and evaluate where hyperspectral imaging creates the most value across food, packaging, health science and quality control.
  • Bringing innovation to the factory floor by designing a standardized quality-control approach and supporting the deployment of hyperspectral imaging in a real manufacturing environment.
  • Sharing your results and recommendations with scientists and business partners, informing future technology choices while gaining exposure to projects across Nestlé categories.

What will make you successful

  • Being a recent graduate in imaging, spectroscopy, data science, physics, chemistry, engineering, microbiology, or a related field
  • Bringing a hands-on mindset coupled with a real enthusiasm for experimenting and building, both in the lab and in code.
  • Having practical skills in image processing and programming, and the digital agility to learn new tools quickly and turn complex data into clear insights.
  • Being curious, proactive and a true team player who enjoys working across disciplines, communicates findings with clarity and stays solution-oriented.
  • Communicating fluently in English
  • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.

Eligibility criteria

  • Being a recent graduate from a Swiss higher education institution (bachelor's or master's degree completed within last 12 months).
  • Swiss nationals, EU/EFTA nationals, or C permit holders only.

At Nestlé Research, we place people at the heart of everything we do

Our benefits are designed to support your growth and wellbeing, so you can thrive at work and in life:

  • Flexible working options, including hybrid work possibilities (for office-based roles)
  • Learning and development opportunities, including mentoring and coaching
  • Paid holidays beyond legal requirements
  • Healthcare contribution and access to the Employee Assistance Program
  • Attractive employee discounts across various products and services

What to expect from our hiring process

We are committed to an inclusive and respectful hiring experience and are happy to provide reasonable accommodations if needed. Our workplaces are fully accessible and supported by a strong safety culture, with modern installations and procedures to ensure a safe environment for everyone.

The hiring process typically includes an interview with Talent Acquisition, an interview with the hiring team, and a final discussion and offer. We review applications as they are received and close the role once a strong list of relevant candidates is identified, so we encourage you to apply early. All information is treated confidentially.

Stay vigilant: Nestlé will only contact candidates via official company email addresses and will never ask for payments or sensitive personal information at any stage of the hiring process.
