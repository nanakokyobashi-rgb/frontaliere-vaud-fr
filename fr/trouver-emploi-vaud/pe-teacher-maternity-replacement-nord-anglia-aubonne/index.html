Founded in 2008, La Côte International School is a co-educational, English-language-based day school for students aged 3 to 18.

Situated in Aubonne, Switzerland—within easy reach of Geneva and Lausanne—our school is home to nearly 450 students representing a variety of cultures and backgrounds.

Through exclusive collaborations with Juilliard, MIT, and UNICEF, we deliver enriching opportunities that inspire students to thrive academically, creatively, and personally.
