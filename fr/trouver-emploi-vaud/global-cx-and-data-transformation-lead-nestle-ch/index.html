Be a force for good and accelerate change that improves lives and the planet

As a Global CX and Data Transformation Lead at Nestlé, your mission is to define and lead Nestlé’s global consumer data strategy, turning data into actionable intelligence that drives growth and measurable business outcomes. You will make a difference by collaborating closely with Global Marketing, technology teams, strategic partners, business services teams, and regional and market leaders. This is your opportunity to take ownership, grow your expertise, and deliver meaningful results that make a difference at scale.

How you’ll make an impact as a Global CX and Data Transformation Lead

  • Defining and leading Nestlé’s global consumer data strategy, turning first-party data into a lasting source of growth and competitive advantage.
  • Shaping the future of Nestlé’s consumer data platform to create a unified view of consumers and enable more relevant, personalised experiences.
  • Owning the global consumer relationship management strategy to deepen consumer relationships and increase lifetime value.
  • Driving artificial intelligence-powered consumer growth and conversational experiences that deliver measurable business impact.
  • Building strategic partnerships and influencing senior leaders, technology providers, and agencies to align around long-term consumer data priorities.
  • Establishing data governance and standards that help markets grow their data maturity while protecting consumer privacy.

What will make you successful

  • Bringing deep expertise in consumer data strategy, consumer relationship management, and digital transformation, with a strong grasp of first-party data, identity, governance, consent, and privacy.
  • Applying solid knowledge of consumer data platforms and marketing technology to connect data, activation, and personalisation at scale.
  • Leading cross-functional teams and influencing senior stakeholders to turn data strategy into business and commercial outcomes.
  • Using knowledge of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and audience modelling to shape future-ready consumer engagement.
  • Communicating fluently in English.
  • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.

What to expect from our hiring process

We are committed to an inclusive and respectful hiring experience and are happy to provide reasonable accommodations if needed. Our workplaces are fully accessible and supported by a strong safety culture, with modern installations and procedures to ensure a safe environment for everyone.

The hiring process typically includes an interview with Talent Acquisition, an interview with the hiring team, and a final discussion and offer. We review applications as they are received and close the role once a strong list of relevant candidates is identified, so we encourage you to apply early. All information is treated confidentially.

Stay vigilant: Nestlé will only contact candidates via official company email addresses and will never ask for payments or sensitive personal information at any stage of the hiring process.
