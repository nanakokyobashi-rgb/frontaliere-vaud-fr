Description complète

Logitech is the Sweet Spot for people who want their actions to have a positive global impact while having the flexibility to do it in their own way.

Hybrid role: 2 days per week min onsite in Lausanne

The Team

Our Global Corporate Communications team connects Logitech with the world. We tell the stories of our innovation, our commitment to sustainability and our culture of authentic passion. We operate in a fast-paced, creative, and highly collaborative environment where big ideas are welcomed and every voice matters.

The Role

The Owned Media Specialist, Global Communications is responsible for content creation, project management, channel management and operational support for Logitech’s owned platforms (i.e., LinkedIn, blog, newsroom and corporate website). Reporting to the head of owned media within the corporate communications team, the specialist will collaborate closely with the broader global communications team, BG social and creator teams and agency partners.

Key Responsibilities

Owned Media

  • Work with the head of owned media to ideate brand communications concepts, as well as prioritize and create compelling content for owned channels and programs.
  • Draft, schedule, and publish posts for owned channels, particularly LinkedIn and the corporate blog, ensuring alignment with core company messaging.
  • Actively monitor owned social channels to engage with our audience and foster community.
  • Update and maintain corporate narratives and initiatives on the website’s corporate pages.
  • Implement and maintain AEO and SEO best practices to continuously drive organic search traffic and improve discoverability.
  • Maintain and distribute a centralized cultural and editorial calendar to align cross-functional teams.
  • Work with creative teams and/or agency partners to create owned content including videos, presentations, infographics and motion graphics.
  • Capture, produce, and edit multimedia content (photography and short-form video) optimized for social media.

Company Narratives and Brand Storytelling

  • Ensure cohesive messaging and alignment across a wider ecosystem of brand channels, including email newsletters, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.
  • Work with the employee communications team to support and amplify employee advocacy and brand champion initiatives.
  • Manage ongoing updates to ensure accuracy on Logitech and Logitech G Wikipedia pages.
  • Work with the broader communications team to update and maintain core media assets, including company decks, b-roll, backgrounders, milestone timelines, and videos.

Operations

  • Track channel metrics and utilize social listening tools (e.g., Sprout Social, Talkwalker, Profound and analytics dashboards) to extract actionable insights, manage brand reputation and implement data-driven improvements against KPIs.
  • Working with the head of owned media, help manage day-to-day relationships with external agencies and write creative briefs for content creation.
  • Handle administrative operations including vendor onboarding, invoice processing, and SOW management.
  • Develop, maintain, and optimize internal systems for tracking projects and deliverables.
  • Continuously monitor and identify industry best practices and emerging trends for owned platforms and content strategy.

Key Qualifications

  • 3+ years of experience in communications, digital marketing or content strategy.
  • Exceptional writing, editing, and storytelling skills, with the proven ability to adapt tone and format for different digital platforms.
  • Proficiency in social listening and analytics tools (e.g., Sprout Social, Talkwalker) with the ability to translate data into actionable content insights.
  • Solid understanding of foundational AEO and SEO principles, as well as organic growth strategies for editorial content.
  • Fluency with AI to streamline workflows, optimize content creation and improve operational processes.
  • Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage editorial calendars, track multiple ongoing projects, and maintain structured workflows on platforms such as Asana.
  • Demonstrated ability to capture, produce, and edit multimedia content (photography and short-form video) natively for social media.
  • Ability to work cross-functionally with different teams, business groups and external agencies in a highly matrixed organization.
  • Detail-oriented with experience handling administrative tasks such as vendor onboarding, SOWs and invoice tracking.
  • A proactive, self-starter mentality capable of thriving in a fast-paced, highly creative global environment.
