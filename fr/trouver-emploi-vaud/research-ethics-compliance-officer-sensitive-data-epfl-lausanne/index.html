Description complète

Mission

The EPFL Research Office (ReO) is seeking a Research Ethics Compliance Officer to support EPFL’s research community with ethics compliance and governance aspects of research projects that are within the scope of the Swiss Federal Act on Research involving Human Beings and the Federal Data Protection Act. This position will also support the establishment and implementation of (i) EPFL's Trusted Research Environment (TRE) — an ISO 27001-certified infrastructure for the processing of sensitive data, with a focus on health-related data — and (ii) the institutional biobank.

The candidate is an individual with proven competence in ethics compliance related to scientific research (incl. with health-related data and human samples). She/he will have excellent communication skills and the ability to work in a dynamic team.

Main duties and responsibilities

Support EPFL principal investigators and ReO Programme Managers in ensuring ethics and regulatory compliance

  • Advise researchers on the applicable ethics and regulatory framework for research involving human participants, human biological samples, and personal/sensitive data.
  • Assess the applicability of the Swiss Federal Act on Research involving Human Beings (HRA) and its Ordinances, the Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), and other Swiss or international requirements, in collaboration with Legal Affairs.
  • Guide researchers through appropriate ethics approval procedures and support applications to Cantonal ethics committees.
  • Review submissions for quality and completeness and support amendments, renewals and project closures.
  • Act as point of contact between researchers and Cantonal ethics committees or other relevant authorities (FOPH, FOEN, etc.) and support follow-up on their requirements.

Develop guidance documents, resources, training and awareness-raising initiatives

  • Contribute to institutional procedures and SOPs for research involving human participants, human biological samples, and personal and sensitive data, particularly health-related data.
  • Develop practical guidance and resources and promote good practices in human research through training and awareness-raising activities.

Provide support on specific ethics compliance and regulatory questions

  • Provide tailored advice on ethics and regulatory questions throughout the lifecycle of projects within the scope of the HRA, FADP.
  • Advise on regulatory aspects of human biological sample transfers and redirect researchers to Legal Affairs and TTO for relevant agreements.
  • Coordinate with Legal Affairs, TTO, DPO, Library, IT services, Biosafety officers and DSPS on cross-cutting regulatory matters.
  • Contribute to institutional ethics compliance procedures and positions on emerging regulatory questions, in collaboration with relevant services.

Provide ethics compliance input and support for the development and implementation of EPFL’s institutional framework for the management of sensitive research data (focus: health-related data) and biological samples

  • Contribute to the roadmap for EPFL’s sensitive/human research data framework, in collaboration with the Institutional Coordinator Human Research Infrastructure and relevant institutional units (Legal Affairs, DPO, IT, etc.).
  • Develop internal governance frameworks, workflows/SOPs, tools and guidance for the ethics/legal compliance of projects involving sensitive research data and/or biological samples.
  • Ensure ethics compliance and governance requirements for EPFL’s biobank infrastructure, including governance regulations, consent documentation and procedures, and certification processes (e.g. SBP Label or ISO).
  • Obtain ethics authorization for the EPFL biobank infrastructure, including preparation and submission of the application and interactions with the Cantonal Ethics Committee.

Support the activities of the EPFL Human Research Ethics Committee (HREC)

  • Provide regulatory and administrative support for the review and follow-up of research protocols involving humans that fall outside the HRA but require HREC approval.

Support other tasks within the ReO/Ethics Affairs

  • Maintain an institutional overview of HRA protocols where EPFL acts as sponsor, partner or participant, including authorizations, amendments, renewals and closures.
  • Collaborate with relevant internal services on cross-cutting ethics compliance and regulatory matters.
  • Support other Ethics Affairs activities as required.

Profile

  • Master’s degree in a relevant field, such as law (specialization human research), public health, life sciences, biomedical sciences or related field (additional certifications and licenses in relation to ethics compliance, biobanking and data protection will be considered a strong asset).
  • Solid knowledge and expertise about the ethics compliance framework and of the ethics compliance and governance requirements in Switzerland (Federal Act on Research involving Human Beings and the Federal Data Protection Act (FADP) and their application).
  • Knowledge about management of human biological samples, human subjects/clinical research data, clinical trials, and biobanks.
  • Extensive experience with the preparation and submission of ethics applications to the Cantonal Ethics Committees.
  • Ability to analyse and understand research projects in relation to the Federal Act on Research involving Human Beings and the FADP.
  • Ability to explain the ethics compliance regulations on research involving humans, personal/sensitive data, biological samples, etc. to researchers who are not necessarily familiar with this area of work.
  • Experience in preparing and developing ethics compliance related guidance documents and tools/resources for a broad audience.
  • Excellent collaboration skills to conduct joint projects and to liaise with internal and external stakeholders (such as legal affairs, cantonal ethics commissions, etc.).
  • Good organization skills, and the ability to set priorities and meet deadlines.
  • The ability to work both independently and in a team, and to adapt to the individual needs of EPFL principal investigators.
  • Excellent collaboration skills to conduct joint projects and to liaise with internal and external stakeholders (such as legal affairs, DPO, IT, Cantonal ethics commissions, etc.).
  • Strong writing, editing and proofreading skills in English.
