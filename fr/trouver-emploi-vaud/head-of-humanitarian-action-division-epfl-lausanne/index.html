Description complète

Mission

The EPFL EssentialTech Centre mobilises science and technology to address challenges of extreme vulnerability through research, education and innovation across sustainable development, humanitarian action and peace.

Main duties and responsibilities

We are seeking an entrepreneurial and scientifically credible leader to develop and lead our Humanitarian Division. As a member of the Centre’s leadership team, reporting to the Centre Director, you will shape the division’s strategy, portfolio and partnerships, connecting EPFL research capabilities with priority challenges identified by humanitarian organisations and affected communities.

The role builds on established initiatives, including Engineering Humanitarian Action (EHA), and combines strategic leadership with hands-on collaboration with EPFL laboratories, researchers and students. The objective is to translate scientific excellence into responsible, context-appropriate solutions with sustained humanitarian impact.

  • Define and implement the Humanitarian Division’s strategy, operational priorities and research and innovation portfolio.
  • Identify pressing humanitarian needs and translate them into collaborative research and innovation opportunities with EPFL laboratories and humanitarian partners.
  • Lead and develop needs-driven partnerships with humanitarian organisations, academic institutions, foundations and other relevant actors, ensuring meaningful engagement of affected communities.
  • Oversee programmes and projects from problem definition through validation and pathways to implementation; promote responsible innovation, Do No Harm, local ownership and long-term sustainability.
  • Develop and implement a fundraising and resource-mobilisation strategy, including competitive proposals and philanthropic partnerships.
  • Initiate and facilitate applied research, contribute to peer-reviewed publications and technical reports, and promote the exchange of scientific and operational knowledge.
  • Contribute to teaching, student supervision, professional training and capacity-building activities.
  • Represent the Centre in relevant scientific and humanitarian forums and contribute to its visibility and communications.
  • Collaborate closely with the Centre’s Sustainable Development and PeaceTech divisions and serve as a resource for the EPFL Laboratories and wider community.

Profile

  • Master’s degree in a STEM discipline (required); PhD in a STEM discipline is an advantage.
  • Substantial experience in the humanitarian sector, including both field and headquarters contexts, with a sound understanding of humanitarian operations and challenges.
  • Demonstrated ability to connect scientific or technological research with humanitarian needs, and proven experience managing interdisciplinary technical or research projects.
  • Experience leading teams or complex collaborative initiatives, with evidence of successful partnerships and resource mobilisation.
  • A strong professional network in humanitarian and/or philanthropic ecosystems, in Switzerland and internationally.
  • Full professional proficiency in French and English (required).
  • Strategic vision, entrepreneurial initiative and a strong commitment to practical societal impact.
  • Integrity, sound judgement and sensitivity to ethical risks and the perspectives of affected communities.
  • Excellent leadership, communication and interpersonal skills; diplomatic, adaptable and able to mobilise diverse stakeholders.
  • Ability to combine long-term thinking with pragmatic delivery and to work collegially across the Centre and EPFL.

We offer

The opportunity to lead a distinctive humanitarian research and innovation division within a leading science and technology institution, working with established humanitarian partners to turn research into meaningful, sustainable impact.

At EPFL, we believe that people achieve great results when they feel supported, respected and enabled to succeed. By joining our community, you will help shape the future of society through education, research and innovation.

We offer an inspiring, international and collaborative environment where your work has real impact and your ideas are valued. Combined with flexible ways of working and attractive employment conditions, we aim to create an environment where everyone can contribute, grow and make a difference.

Beyond work, our campuses host a vibrant community and unique environment, enriched by initiatives promoting equality, inclusion, diversity, well-being and sustainability as well as an engaging program of cultural, sports and community activities throughout the year.

Questions sur cette offre

Quel salaire propose EPFL pour ce poste ?

L'annonce d'origine n'indique pas de salaire. Notre estimation pour ce rôle et ce secteur à Lausanne est de CHF 83'500 - 126'500 bruts par an — une estimation, pas un montant communiqué par EPFL. Le calculateur de ce site convertit un salaire brut en net réel une fois l'impôt frontalier et les cotisations sociales appliqués.

Est-ce un poste à temps plein, et quel type de contrat propose EPFL ?

Cette offre est un poste en temps plein. Le type de contrat affiché ici provient directement de l'annonce originale de l'employeur ; confirmez toujours les horaires exacts, le préavis et la durée d'essai avec EPFL pendant le processus de candidature, ces détails pouvant varier même au sein de la même catégorie de contrat.

Ai-je besoin d'un permis de frontalier pour un poste dans le canton de Vaud ?

Les citoyens UE/AELE résidant dans un État UE/AELE peuvent demander un permis G et travailler dans toute la Suisse : les zones frontalières ne s'appliquent plus à eux, et ils doivent rentrer au moins une fois par semaine. L'employeur suisse dépose la demande auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Vaud après signature du contrat. Les délais varient selon le canton — confirmez les détails auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Vaud ou avec les RH pendant la candidature.

Comment postuler à ce poste chez EPFL ?
