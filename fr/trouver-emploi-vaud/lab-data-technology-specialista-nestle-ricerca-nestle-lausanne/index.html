Strategic Research Operations Location: Lausanne, Switzerland Work model: Hybrid / Full time 100% Contract type / duration: Permanent

Shape the future through scientific breakthroughs and innovation.

As a Lab Data Technology Specialist at Nestlé Research within our Computational Sciences department, you will be responsible for reinforcing our laboratory data foundations to help unlock the full value of the data generated by our scientific instruments and laboratory systems.

  • Strategic Research Operations Location: Lausanne, Switzerland Work model: Hybrid / Full time 100% Contract type / duration: Permanent
  • Shape the future through scientific breakthroughs and innovation.

Processus de candidature

  • Integrating lab instruments, applications, robots, and IoT with reference databases and data warehouses (e.g., Snowflake).
  • Solid grasp of the lab solutions ecosystem: ELN, CDS, LES, multi-omics pipelines, IoT-connected equipment, barcode systems, etc.
  • Programming (e.g., Python, R, Java, Groovy).
  • Data engineering and modeling — ETL/ELT (Airflow, dbt), APIs, orchestration, SQL/NoSQL — with engineering rigor (Git, CI/CD, testing, data quality, observability, reproducibility). Nice-to-have technical skills
  • Semantic modeling, ontologies, and knowledge graphs (e.g., RDF/SPARQL, biomedical/chemical vocabularies).
  • FAIR data implementation, metadata management, and lineage tooling
  • Enabling AI/ML and analytics use cases on scientific data
  • Domain exposure in chemistry, biology, food, or omics
