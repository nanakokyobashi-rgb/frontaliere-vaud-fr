Description complète

As a Compliance Officer - Onboarding, you will be part of the Compliance Department and work in the Compliance Onboarding, Reviews & Advisory Team. Your responsibilities will be primarily focused on Onboarding related matters and the associated Advisory role. You will also be working in close collaboration with the Reviews team and contribute to the mission of the Compliance Department, acting as a second line of defense. Key responsibilities Onboarding

  • Conduct ex-ante reviews of all account opening requests involving High-Risk Relationships (HRR) clients and Politically Exposed Persons (PEP) (private clients, corporate structures such as, but not limited to, operating, domiciliary companies, trusts, collective investments schemes, external asset managers, as well as financial institutions), prior to account activation:
  • Assess the overall client onboarding file and its supporting documentation, conducting due diligence verifications as required
  • Review the HRR memorandum prepared by the first line of defense (1LoD) in order to submit a recommendation to the Executive Management (EM) and/or the relevant AML decision-making body
  • Be able to defend the Compliance position within the relevant AML decision- making body
  • Work in close coordination with the Business Risk Management (BRM) or Client Lifecycle Integrity (CLI) departments, which are part of the first line of defense

Reviews

  • Conduct ex-ante reviews of periodic/ongoing reviews involving High-Risk Relationships (HRR) clients and Politically Exposed Persons (PEP) (private clients, corporate structures such as, but not limited to, operating, domiciliary companies, trusts, collective investments schemes, external asset managers, as well as financial institutions)
  • Assess the supporting file and the review materials prepared by the 1LoD and submit recommendations for EM approval.
  • In addition to the assessment to be performed for onboarding requests, periodic review requests require a transactional activity analysis of the file.

Advisory & Support

  • Provide advisory support to the BRM, CLI, Front Office departments regarding account opening requests requiring Compliance input as well as general Compliance matters
  • Provide guidance on complex questions arising both from the internal compliance framework and external regulatory requirements
  • Provide guidance on the application of the risk tolerance framework as defined by the Board of Directors to the BRM and CLI teams
  • Contribute to drafting, and optimizing, internal memos and procedures
  • Participate in key regulatory projects
  • Contribute to providing AML training sessions to the Front Office, BRM and CLI departments

Qualifications

  • Bachelor or Master Degree, preferably in Laws or in Economics
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in client onboarding, KYC/AML, or compliance within the financial sector
  • Strong Knowledge of Swiss regulatory framework (AMLA, AMLO-FINMA, CDB) and other applicable regulations
  • Strong knowledge of onboarding due diligence standards
  • Excellent command of English and French (another language is a strong asset)
  • Ability to multi-task and work under pressure
  • Analytical and methodological approach
  • Very good communication skills
  • Strong team player who enjoys working in multicultural, dynamic, technological and international environment
