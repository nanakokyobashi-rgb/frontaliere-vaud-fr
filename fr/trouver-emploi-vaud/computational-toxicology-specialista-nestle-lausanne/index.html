Shape the future through scientific breakthroughs and innovation

As a Computational Toxicology Specialist at Nestlé Research, your mission is to apply predictive in silico approaches that strengthen chemical risk assessment and safety by design, helping ensure the food safety of products enjoyed by millions of consumers. You will make a difference by collaborating closely with food safety, quality, research and regulatory experts across Nestlé, as well as universities, food safety institutes and international scientific organizations. This is your opportunity to turn ideas into sustainable and innovative solutions for people, pets, and the planet.

How you’ll make an impact as a Computational Toxicology Specialist

  • Advancing Next Generation Risk Assessment (NGRA) by integrating computational predictions with toxicological evidence to support scientifically robust chemical safety assessments and by evaluating in silico methodologies as alternatives to experimental testing, including their predictive performance, biological relevance, applicability domains and suitability for regulatory use.
  • Performing and interpreting robust computational safety assessments of contaminants, food contact materials, plant constituents and other substances relevant to food safety, to protect consumers and enable innovation, using approaches such as structure–activity modelling, read-across, structural alerts and molecular property predictions to support clear, scientifically sound safety decisions.
  • Accelerating innovation and research projects through timely computational toxicology expertise , addressing rapid-response safety questions with timely and high-quality scientific reports and recommendations.
  • Developing and strengthening computational toxicology capabilities by collaborating with experts to improve the toxicological relevance and applicability of predictive models, workflows, databases and knowledge systems, making chemical and toxicological data easier to access, trust and reuse.
  • Developing expertise in computational toxicology and NGRA through scientific collaboration, knowledge sharing, publications and participation in scientific networks.

What will make you successful

  • Bringing a PhD in Toxicology, complemented by hands-on experience in computational toxicology, and integrating in silico and in vitro data, to enable human health hazard and chemical risk assessment.
  • Experience with (Q)SAR modelling, read-across, predictive toxicology tools (e.g., Derek/Sarah Nexus, OECD QSAR Toolbox, VEGA or equivalent tools), gained through academic research or professional experience, would be an advantage.
  • Using a strong scientific, problem-solving mindset to connect science, risk assessment and business decisions; understanding of internal exposure and physiologically based kinetic modelling is a plus.
  • Collaborating across research, quality and regulatory teams, with excellent communication and influencing skills adapted to each audience.
  • Bringing curiosity, creativity and initiative to stay ahead of emerging developments in artificial intelligence, machine learning and predictive toxicology.
  • Basic coding or scripting skills (e.g., Python) and a critical, open-minded interest in AI and machine-learning methods, with attention to data quality, validation and fitness for safety assessment.
  • Communicating confidently in English, spoken and written; any additional language is an asset.
  • Bringing collaboration, fresh thinking, bold questions, respect, and a positive, wellbeing-oriented mindset, to create shared value.
